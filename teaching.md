<h1>Supervision &amp; teaching</h1>

<div class="prose">
  <p class="lead">Explaining a measurement to someone who does not share your training is the same work as making it: you find out what you actually understand. I teach physics to biologists, and I build the material from scratch when none exists.</p>
</div>

  <h2 class="section-title">Interdisciplinary training</h2>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>Courses and workshops for working researchers, on either side of the physics–biology divide. Neither undergraduate teaching nor public outreach: the audience already does research, and what is being passed on is a way of measuring.</p>
  </div>
  <ul class="conf-list">
    <li>
      <span class="conf-year">2026</span>
      <span><strong>Rheology of cells and tissues</strong> — <em>AQV interdisciplinary online courses</em>, GDR 2108 <span lang="fr">Approches Quantitatives du Vivant</span>, the French community for quantitative biology. A 20-minute introduction followed by 40 minutes of open questions, aimed at biologists who work on mechanical phenomena and would rather face rheological jargon than avoid it. 6 November 2026.</span>
