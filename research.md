<h1>Research</h1>

<div class="prose">
  <p class="question">A physical model of a tissue is only worth the measurement that challenges it.</p>
  <p class="lead">I am an experimentalist working on the mechanics of living tissues, and this conviction is the thread of my work.</p>
  <p>During my PhD I asked how far a minimal model, a few surface and line tensions, goes in explaining the shape of epithelial cells, and found where it breaks. Testing models this way requires measuring stresses and material properties inside living tissues, so my postdoctoral work builds droplet sensors and magnetic rheometers that do it in embryos. My research project brings the two together to ask how cellular heterogeneity and tissue mechanics shape one another.</p>
</div>
